# Kansas Homeschool Requirements

**Bottom line: nothing is required of us this year, or next year.** Kansas is
one of the lowest-regulation homeschool states.

## The rules

- **Compulsory school age in Kansas is 7.** Until then, homeschooling (or not
  schooling at all) is legally optional — no registration, no reporting.
  Our daughter turns 7 in ~2029, so kindergarten and 1st grade at home are
  completely paperwork-free.
- **When she turns 7:** Kansas classifies homeschools as **Non-Accredited
  Private Schools (NAPS)**. We register once with the State Board of
  Education — a free online form with the school's name/address and the
  custodian of records (Brandy). That's it.
  ([KSDE official page](https://www.ksde.gov/student-success/learning-alternatives/nonaccredited-private-schools))
- **No testing, no curriculum approval, no progress monitoring, no
  inspections.** The instructor must be "competent," but no teaching license
  is required.
- Instruction expectation once compulsory age applies: substantially
  equivalent to public school (~186 days / ~1,116 hours for grades 1–11) —
  loosely defined and not monitored.

## Fun fact

Registering as a NAPS means Brandy gets to *name her school*. Worth a family
brainstorm when the time comes.
