# Little Hearts for His Glory — Trial Week Shopping List

What Unit 1, Days 1–5 (the free "First Week of Plans" sample) actually
requires, and what to buy vs. substitute. The sample PDF itself *is* the
teacher's guide for week 1, so no guide purchase is needed to trial it.

## Worth buying (the daily core, ~$40–60 total)

| Book | Used | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **History for Little Pilgrims** | every day | The core history reader — the spine of the left page |
| **The Things God Made** | every day | "Picture the Past" read-aloud + discussion |
| **The World God Made** | Days 1, 4, 5 | Science "text connection" |
| **Do It Carefully** + **Going On Eagerly** (Rod & Staff) | Days 1, 3 | Thinking-skills/fine-motor workbooks — cheap (~$4 each) |
| *(optional)* History for Little Pilgrims **Coloring Book** | Days 1, 4, 5 | Companion coloring pages |

All available at [heartofdakota.com](https://www.heartofdakota.com/little-hearts-for-his-glory/)
(also Rainbow Resource / Christianbook for some titles).

## Free or already owned — substitute, don't buy

| Slot | The plans call for | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Language arts read-aloud | *The Adventures of Reddy Fox* | Free — [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/12137) |
| Bible story (Days 4–5) | Family-Time Bible OR 101 Favorite Stories OR "your own Bible" | Our own Bible — explicitly allowed |
| Scripture memory | "Scripture Card 1" (Matt 19:14) | Index card |
| Music | *Hide 'Em in Your Heart* Vol. 1, Track 1 (Steve Green) | Stream it (Spotify/YouTube) |
| Math | Essential K Math A/B **or** Singapore Primary 1A/1B (choose-one slot) | Our existing Singapore books |
| Phonics | Choose-one menu (Reading Made Easy, The Reading Lesson, etc.) | Skip for the trial — use our letter-of-the-week pages |
| Handwriting (Days 2, 4) | A Reason for Handwriting K OR Getty & Dubay Italic A | Our tracing printables ("own program" is a listed option) |
| Storytime | *Danny the Workhorse* + Pleasant Valley Farm Coloring Book | Any favorite picture book, or double up on Reddy Fox |
| "God's Creation" rhyme | Guide appendix (not in sample) | Skip or make up motions |

## Notes

- Every "choose one" box (phonics, math, handwriting) is designed for
  substitution — using our own materials there is running the program as
  intended, not cheating the trial.
- If the trial succeeds, the full purchase decision is package tier +
  phonics pick + math level; the core books above are all part of the
  package anyway, so nothing bought for the trial is wasted.
- Supplies used in week 1: flashlight, beanbag/sock ball, brown + blue
  paper, black/gray/red/yellow paper, glue, crayons, a stuffed animal.
