## Questions for Brandy

### Vision
1. When you picture a *great* homeschool morning a year from now, what's
   happening? (Workbooks at the table? Reading on the couch? Messy crafts?
   Outside?)
2. What matters most this first year — reading readiness, love of learning,
   Bible knowledge, routine, something else?

### Faith
3. Do you want Bible woven into every subject, or academics + a separate
   family Bible time we design ourselves?

### Practical
4. How much time per day feels right? (At 4½, most experienced homeschoolers
   say 30–60 minutes of "seat work" max, plus lots of reading aloud and play.)
5. What's our budget for curriculum this year?
6. What will the once-a-week program cover, so home days complement rather
   than repeat it?
7. How should the younger two fit in — do you want materials/activities that
   can include them, or plans for occupying them during lessons?

### Logistics
8. What does our state require for a 4/5-year-old? (In most states,
   compulsory school age is 6+, so this year is likely paperwork-free — worth
   confirming for ours.)
