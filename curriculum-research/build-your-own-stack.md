# Option B: The Build-Your-Own Stack
### Treehouse Schoolhouse + Logic of English + Singapore Math

We already own and have used 1–2 Singapore pre-K workbooks, and we're drawn to
Treehouse Schoolhouse and Logic of English. Those three actually snap together
into a complete, coherent curriculum — an alternative to buying one all-in-one
box (MFW / Heart of Dakota).

## The pieces

### Treehouse Schoolhouse — the "heart" subjects
[treehouseschoolhouse.com](https://treehouseschoolhouse.com/) — a Christian,
Charlotte Mason/Waldorf-inspired publisher (carried by
[Christianbook](https://www.christianbook.com/page/homeschool/treehouse-schoolhouse)).
Beautiful, seasonal, family-style materials. **Key nuance: the Scripture
content varies by product**, so pick deliberately:

| Product | Ages | Scripture? | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Treehouse Nature Study](https://treehouseschoolhouse.com/collections/treehouse-nature-study) | preschool–middle school | Yes — Biblical truth woven in | Seasonal nature study: living books, notebooking, poetry, picture study, folk songs. Designed for multiple ages at once |
| [Bible collection](https://treehouseschoolhouse.com/collections/bible) (A Year of Memory Work, Advent/Easter studies) | all | Yes — it's the point | Scripture memory + hands-on Bible with art and discussion — exactly the "Bible with activities and breakdowns" Brandy asked for |
| [Story School: Preschool](https://treehouseschoolhouse.com/products/treehouse-story-school-preschool) | 2.5–5 | **No** — deliberately no religious content in this one | 32 weeks, one picture book per week: music, movement, alphabet/number play, crafts, baking |

Treehouse covers Bible, nature/science, and beauty subjects — but it is
**not** a phonics or math program. That's where the other two come in.

### Logic of English Foundations A — reading
[Foundations](https://logicofenglish.com/blogs/help-center/foundations-with-pre-k-students)
is an Orton-Gillingham-inspired phonics/reading/handwriting program for ages
4–7 (A & B ≈ kindergarten year). Highly rated
([Cathy Duffy](https://cathyduffyreviews.com/homeschool-reviews-core-curricula/phonics-reading/phonics-reading-programs/foundations-logic-of-english-levels-ad),
[detailed review](https://smarterlearningguide.com/logic-of-english-foundations-review/)).
Fit notes:

- **Multisensory and game-based** — taught through movement and games, not
  worksheet drill. This is the strongest match in the phonics world for
  "interactive, not just workbooks."
- No sight-word memorization; real phonics rules from day one. Kids taught
  this way tend to become strong spellers.
- Age 4 is the recommended floor — she's exactly in the window, and Level A
  starts from zero.
- Religiously neutral (not Christian, not anti-) — fine, since Bible comes
  from Treehouse in this stack.
- One caveat: it's teacher-led, ~4 lessons/week. More prep than
  open-and-go boxes, though the lessons themselves are scripted.

### Singapore Math — math (continuity!)
We've already done Singapore pre-K workbooks and she liked them. Natural next
step: **Singapore Dimensions Math KA/KB** (the modern kindergarten level, with
hands-on activities) or Essential Math K. Sticking with Singapore keeps her in
one mathematical "language" from pre-K through elementary — its
concrete→pictorial→abstract approach is exactly the interactive style Brandy
wants.

## How the week would look

- **Morning basket:** Treehouse Bible/memory work (all three kids together)
- **Table time 1:** Logic of English Foundations A (big kid) — little sister
  does Story School: Preschool alongside
- **Table time 2:** Singapore Dimensions KA
- **Hands-on time:** Treehouse Nature Study (genuinely multi-age — both girls)
- Enrichment school day (Wed): nothing at home but read-alouds

Bonus: **Story School: Preschool is aimed at ages 2.5–5 — it could be the
little sister's own curriculum**, solving the tag-along question with
something designed for it (its lack of Bible content doesn't matter, since
Bible happens together in morning basket).

## Stack vs. all-in-one box — the honest trade-off

| | Build-your-own stack | MFW / Heart of Dakota box |
|---|---|---|
| Fit to each subject | Best-in-class per subject | One publisher's take on everything |
| Bible | Treehouse Bible products (activity-rich) | Built into the daily flow |
| Uses what we own | Continues Singapore momentum | Replaces it (boxes include their own math approach) |
| Mom's planning load | She is the conductor — 3 teacher guides to interleave | One guide, open to today's page |
| Style | Charlotte Mason, seasonal, beautiful | MFW: unit-study crafty; HOD: gentle CM |
| Risk | Overbuying / juggling | One weak subject you're stuck with |

## Recommendation

Given (a) Singapore is already working, (b) Brandy asked about Treehouse and
LOE unprompted — meaning they already appeal to her taste — and (c) the stack
maps 1:1 onto the weekly rhythm chart we built, **the stack is now the
front-runner**, with Heart of Dakota as the fallback if conducting three
guides proves to be too many plates to spin. Suggested trial: buy only
LOE Foundations A + Dimensions KA + one Treehouse Bible product first;
add Nature Study and Story School Preschool once the core rhythm holds.
