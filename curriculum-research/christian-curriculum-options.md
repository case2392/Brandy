# Explicitly Christian Curriculum Options (Pre-K / Kindergarten)

Options that are unambiguously Christian (evangelical/Protestant publishers),
for comparison against The Good and the Beautiful. Focused on what fits a
4½–5 year old.

## Strong candidates to look at first

### My Father's World (MFW)
- Gospel-centered; blends Charlotte Mason + classical + unit studies
- Their **"God's Creation from A to Z"** kindergarten program is hands-on and
  craft-heavy — often named one of the best K programs for tactile learners
- Gentle pace, designed for exactly this age
- [mfwbooks.com](https://www.mfwbooks.com)

### Sonlight
- Literature-based (learning through great read-aloud books, not worksheets)
- Openly evangelical Christian for 30+ years; Bible is a core subject
- Pre-K/K packages are mostly reading aloud together — very natural at 4½
- Pricier, but you get a big library of real books out of it
- [Their own comparison of Christian programs](https://blog.sonlight.com/best-christian-homeschool-programs.html)

### Apologia (Kindergarten)
- Known for creation-based science; now has a full K program
- Described as open-and-go and parent-friendly for first-time homeschoolers
- [Apologia kindergarten](https://www.apologia.com/grade-level/elementary/kindergarten/)

## How to choose (the real fork in the road)

The biggest differentiator isn't "which is most Christian" — it's **teaching
style**, because Brandy is the one living with it every day:

1. **Open-and-go workbooks** (CLE, Horizons, Abeka): minimal prep, clear
   checklist feeling. Good if she wants structure and visible progress.
2. **Literature/experience-based** (Sonlight, MFW, Heart of Dakota,
   Masterbooks): snuggle-on-the-couch reading, crafts, nature walks. Good if
   she wants gentle and relational — the usual recommendation at this age.
